UGC DEEMED-TO-BE-UNIVERSITY APPLICATION NORMS
Regulation: UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023


============================================================
1. GENERAL CATEGORY
============================================================

NORM: Minimum Number of Departments
VALUE: 5 departments

NORM: Minimum Academic Programmes
VALUE: 5 programmes

NORM: Minimum NAAC Accreditation Score
VALUE: 3.01 CGPA

NORM: NAAC Accreditation Cycles
VALUE: 3 consecutive cycles

NORM: NBA Accredited Programme Requirement
VALUE: At least 2/3 of eligible technical programmes

NORM: NBA Accreditation Cycles
VALUE: 3 consecutive cycles

NORM: NIRF Category Ranking
VALUE: Top 50 in the relevant category

NORM: NIRF Category Ranking Duration
VALUE: 3 consecutive years

NORM: NIRF Overall Ranking
VALUE: Top 100 overall

NORM: NIRF Overall Ranking Duration
VALUE: 3 consecutive years

NORM: Teacher-Student Ratio
VALUE: 1:20

NORM: Minimum Number of Teachers
VALUE: 150 teachers

NORM: Minimum Student Strength
VALUE: 3,000 students

NORM: Minimum PG/Research Student Proportion
VALUE: At least 1/5 of total students

NORM: Teaching Mode
VALUE: Regular classroom mode

NORM: Academic Infrastructure
VALUE: As per UGC / relevant statutory body norms

NORM: Physical Infrastructure
VALUE: As per UGC / relevant statutory body norms

NORM: Professional Programme Approval
VALUE: Approval from relevant statutory body

NORM: Corpus Fund
VALUE: ₹25 Crore

NORM: Corpus Fund Applicability
VALUE: Institutions not funded by Government

NORM: Land Ownership / Lease
VALUE: Sale deed OR minimum 30-year lease

NORM: Strategic Vision Plan
VALUE: 15 years

NORM: Implementation Plan
VALUE: 5-year rolling implementation plan

NORM: DPR
VALUE: Detailed Project Report required

NORM: Financial Sustainability
VALUE: Financial sustainability plan required

NORM: Statutory Compliance
VALUE: Compliance with UGC Act, Rules and 2023 Regulations
